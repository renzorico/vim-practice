## Goal
After the Mason is cured, he asks Tilde to fix his ledger. A plain text buffer, no tiles: the skills of World 2 used on real text, plus two new ones taught here: `o` (open a line below, type, `<Esc>`) and `D` (delete to the end of the line).
Proven with the real engine: from the start lines below, `jdd 13l2xj.j. 0llra jdd 013l2x 4lD jdd odoor<Esc>` (38 keys, spaces only for reading) gives exactly the target. Every command in it is taught by then.
