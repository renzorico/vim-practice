## Why
The game is silent. Every turn is a decision; a sound per outcome tells the player what happened before they read anything (moved, jumped, blocked, deleted, undone, hurt, cleared).

## 1. Cues (a pure function, tested)
`audio/cues.ts`: `cuesFor(events: readonly WorldEvent[], previous: WorldState, next: WorldState): Cue[]`, pure, no Web Audio. Cues:
- `step` (walk, per cell up to 4 then one tail), `jump` (pitch rises with distance), `land` (end of a jump), `bump` (low, short), `fixed` (a dull knock), `delete` (a short crunch; longer for a row), `put` (a soft pop), `undo` (a reverse swish) and `redo` (forward), `hit` (a sting), `death`, `clear` (a 4-note rising jingle), `lever`/`trigger` (a chime when a story trigger fires), `search-miss` (two falling notes).
- Timing: the cue's offset in ms comes from the P-4 timing functions (a jump's `land` at its duration), so sound and motion agree.
- At most 3 cues per turn (priority: hit/death/clear > trigger > edit > movement).

## 2. Synth
`audio/synth.ts`: one lazily created `AudioContext`, resumed on the first key or click (autoplay rules); a master gain (default 0.35); each cue a tiny oscillator/noise recipe (square or triangle waves, short envelopes, 8-bit feel matching the pixel art), under 250 ms except `clear` and `death`. No allocation per frame; nodes are disconnected when done.
- Dialogue: a soft blip every 3 revealed characters during P-7's reveal, pitch per speaker (stable from the speaker id), none for `system`/`scribe` lines (the Scribe's note gets one paper rustle when it opens). Stops when the line is advanced.
- Stage-clear card: one chime per star, staggered.

## 3. Settings
- Sound on by default at low volume; a toggle on the title screen ("Sound: on/off") and a small speaker button in the play footer (`aria-pressed`), saved in `localStorage` (`unsaved.sound.v1`, wrapped in try/catch: blocked storage means on).
- Never bind a letter key to mute (every letter is a Vim key).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` does not mute.
- If Web Audio is unavailable or throws, the game plays silently with no error.

## Tests
- `cuesFor` for: one `l`, `5l` (capped steps), `w` (jump then land at the jump's duration), a bump, `x`, `dd`, `u`, `<C-r>`, a trigger, a heart lost, a death, a clear, a search miss; the 3-cue cap and priority.
- Synth with a fake `AudioContext`: a cue creates and disconnects its nodes; muted creates nothing; missing `AudioContext` does not throw.
- The setting persists; blocked storage defaults to on.
- Existing tests pass (the full-run App test must not need audio).
