## Why (from the design lead's playthrough of a new game)
1. On the Margin's first screen five small people stand on the grass, and nothing says which one is you.
2. The objective says "Talk to the Elder", but nothing shows who or where the Elder is.
3. The HUD shows `Turn 7 · par 6` before the player has even received the first quest: the Margin is a village to walk around and talk in, so par there only says "you're already failing".
4. "Hold Tab for Source" means nothing to a new player.

## 1. You are the cursor
Draw Vim's cursor on the player's cell: a block outline (1 px inside the cell, the World's accent colour) under the player sprite in Normal mode; a 2 px bar on the cell's left edge in Insert mode; an underline in operator-pending mode (after `d`, `c`, `y`). It blinks at 1 Hz (on 600 ms, off 400 ms), resets to on whenever the player moves, and does not blink with reduced motion. It follows the drawn position (P-4's carry-over), not the logical cursor, so it never leads the sprite. Hide it during cutscenes and on text stages (they have their own cursor).

## 2. Where to go
`objectiveTarget(content, story, stage, world): Position | null`, pure: take the main quest's current step (`objectives` order: main first); if its `completeWhen` is a `trigger` condition whose trigger is defined in the current stage, return the cell of that trigger (an `interact` trigger on an entity: the entity's current cell; an `enter` trigger: its cell; a `text` trigger: null). Otherwise null.
- Draw a small down arrow (3 px tall chevron, the accent colour) one cell above the target, bobbing 2 px at 1 Hz (still with reduced motion). If the target is off screen, draw the arrow at the nearest screen edge pointing toward it.
- Not in dark stages (`darknessFor` returns a level): finding things there is the lesson. Not on text stages. Not while a dialogue or card is open.
- Tests: the Margin at start points at the Elder; after the Elder, at Mira; a step completed by `stage-cleared` gives null; a dark stage gives no arrow (scene test).

## 3. No par in the village
The Margin (stage id `margin`) has no par in the HUD, no stars and no best (as text stages since P-6), and its card shows "cleared" and hearts only. Make this a set (`UNSCORED_STAGES`) next to the text-stage rule so later hub stages can join it; the title's total stars excludes it.

## 4. The Tab label
Footer: "Hold Tab to see the file" (instead of "Hold Tab for Source"). Change only that text.
