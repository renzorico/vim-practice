## Why
Renzo: "we need a short intro explaining what is this about before the game starts, like what's gonna be the goal of the game so people get actually hooked... It seems too abrupt and confusing on where to start or where are we."

## 1. The prologue: eight beats, under 45 seconds, skippable
Plays on New game, before the first world banner. Drawn on one canvas with the game's own tiles (integer scale, crisp), text below in a fixed two-row caption (at least 22 px, 4.5:1). Each beat types its caption at 45 cps, then waits for Enter (or auto-advances 3.5 s after the text finishes). Enter while typing completes the caption. `Esc` skips all; a small "Esc skip" is always visible. Text exactly:
1. "Once, someone wrote a world." A dark page, one blinking block cursor; it types rows of plain characters.
2. "Every tree, every house, every road:\na letter on a page." Each character turns into its tile, left to right, row by row, until it is the Margin village.
3. "They called it Folio, and wrote\na little more of it every night." The view pulls back to a long strip: the seven worlds appear one after another, in their palettes.
4. "Then one night the sky went black.\nThe writer never came back." Two flickers to black (one fade with reduced motion); the cursor stops blinking.
5. "Now something grey walks the land.\nIt deletes it, one row at a time." A grey hooded figure crosses; behind it rows become bare paper.
6. "You are Tilde. You live on the last row.\nIt's coming your way." Tilde at the edge of the village, `~` rows beyond; the paper is three rows away.
7. "Find the Scribe. Make them save the world\nbefore there's nothing left to save." The map: seven places on a path, "you" at the Margin, a lit question mark at the far end. Title above: "YOUR GOAL".
8. "You move the way text moves: with keys.\nYou'll learn each one when you need it." `h j k l` keycaps move Tilde four steps. Small line: "It's real Vim. What you learn here works there." Button: "Enter: wake up".
- It never says file, swap, crash or buffer, and never shows the Scribe's face (MASTERPLAN 3.4).
- Reduced motion: each beat is one still picture, text appears whole, no auto-advance.
- Sound: cursor ticks while typing (the existing dialogue blip, quiet), one low tone on beat 4, none when muted.
- Keys pressed here never reach the game; held Enter never runs through more than one beat.
- Title menu gets "Intro" (after "Keys") to watch it again, returning to the title. Not shown on Continue, Stages or replay. No save change.

## 2. Where am I
- World banner: add the map strip from beat 7 (places visited filled, the current one lit, later ones as dark "?"), the world's number and name, and one line of why (this data): margin "Home. What's left of it." lowlands "The Lighthouse has to be warned." quarry "The road south, after the Scribe." wordwood "Through the wood, to the ferry." copper-port "Someone is filling the port with copies." library "The Scribe signed a contract here." echo-caves "The Scribe's study, somewhere in the dark." last-page "The end of the page." Banner time 2.6 s (any key skips, as today).
- HUD: under the stage title, small: "World 1 of 7 · Find the Scribe" (the Margin is "World 0"; the Last Page says only "Find the Scribe").
- Stage list: the same map strip at the top.
