## 1. Regressions from P-15 (first)
- Ground replacement must apply only to natural ground (grass, dirt, sand, field...). Indoor and built floors (Lighthouse, Shaft, Warehouse, Customs, counting house, Library, vault, caves, branches, moss, boards, ledge) keep their own sprite. Classify every ground sprite explicitly; test the list. The Lighthouse is now drawn as open sea: that is the bug to check first.
- Lowlands land reads as water (blue-grey ground, dashes like waves). Make it pale chalk meadow: straw and grey-green, with dry-grass tufts; keep the wiped field bare paper. Objects standing on paper (the stepping stones) have paper under them, not a ground square: what is under an object follows its neighbours (majority of the 4).
- The Cut's chasm reads as a starry sky: draw depth (darker toward the middle, rock lips on the land edge, no dots). The ledge must read as walkable stone.
- Last Page: no grey squares behind spaces; the page is plain paper.
- Glades moss is noisy: cut its contrast by half, so actors and markers stand out.
- Particles stay inside the visible map area; the Lowlands ones must not look like waves.

## 2. Surroundings (no more maps floating in a void)
Outside the map's cells, draw what that place is in, dimmed (about 45% toward the background), static, pooled, behind everything: sea with a few slow foam marks (Lighthouse, Jetty), rock strata (Quarry, Cut, Shaft), forest canopy and trunks (Wordwood), warehouse brick and beams (Port interiors), tall dim bookcases (Library), cave rock (Caves), bare paper (Last Page). It must be obvious it is not walkable: no walkable sprite is used, and a 2 px dark edge follows the map's outline. Enough cells to fill the view at 1920x1080, and no more.

## 3. Light
- A soft vignette per world (one static image on the camera, under the HUD depth).
- Light pools: lamps, windows, the lit Lighthouse lamp, Save Stones and crystals cast a warm additive glow (2 to 3 tiles, pulsing 4% at 0.5 Hz; still with reduced motion).
- Dark stages: replace the hard square steps with a smooth falloff (same radius and the same cells hidden: a cell at alpha 0 stays fully hidden, since finding is the lesson). Wall-top highlights follow the same falloff.

## 4. Tall things
Trees, pillars, shelves at the top of a block, lamps and the Save Stone are drawn 16x24: the upper 8 px overlap the cell above, above the ground and behind actors that stand below, in front of actors that stand above. They never cover a Vim character in source view (Tab hides the overhang), nor a marker or the cursor.
