## The idea (proven in the sim)
The Last Page is the E325 dialog itself. The page is damaged; the Unwriter (kind 'unwriter', behaviour `unwrite`, every 3 turns, always the bottom row: the doors) tears the doors off; `u` brings them back, but only when his tear is the most recent change. `u` at the wrong moment undoes the player's own last repair. That rhythm is the finale: `u` right after he tears, then two turns of repair, then he tears again.
- Repairs, each a World skill and none changing the row count (so he always tears the doors row): `ATTENTOIN` (`fO x p`), the doubled `the the` (`daw`), the swapped `(2)`/`(1)` lines (`ddp`).
- The whole page is a multi-row `text` trigger (`last.whole`) on the margin anchor at (0,0). When it fires, the Unwriter stops: a story reaction removes him ("the Scribe is reading"), so the choice and the save are calm.
- The doors are `enter` triggers on the door letters in the doors row (`onlyIf last.whole`): R (6,17), D (6,28), Q (6,38), A (6,46), O (6,1), E (6,9).
- R, then `:w`: the clear is `last.recover` and `saved` together.
Measured: `fO x p u j fh u daw j u dd p u` makes the page whole at turn 13; `/R<CR>` lands on R at turn 14; `:w` is turn 15. Par 15 (the `solution` in the file). No old-tools comparison: the finale uses everything.
