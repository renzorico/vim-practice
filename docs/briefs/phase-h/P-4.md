## Why
Renzo played it: "the motion of the character feels buggy". The causes, from the code:
1. Every move plays in 60 ms (walk) or 90 ms (`PLAYBACK_BUDGET_MS`, any jump), linear. `30l` crosses 30 cells in 90 ms: it reads as a teleport.
2. `animationSteps` turns any move longer than one cell into a `jump`, so a counted walk (`5j`, `30l`, which the world reports as a walk) hops in an arc.
3. A bump is a 2 px shake for 60 ms: invisible, so a blocked key looks like a dropped key.
4. Every key calls `finish()`, which snaps the player to the last destination before the next move starts; holding or tapping `j` stutters.
5. Player, every entity and the water all flip their idle frame on the same 500 ms tick: the whole map twitches in unison.

This pass is presentation only. The world, the engine and every replay stay exactly as they are.

## What to build
Put the timing rules in pure functions in `animate.ts` (unit-tested), and keep `FieldScene` as the renderer.

1. Walks (`player-moved` with style `walk`, any distance, so counted `hjkl` too; `entity-move`): no arc. Duration `clamp(110 + 40 * (cells - 1), 110, 420)` ms, ease-out (quad). A 1-2 px vertical bob per cell crossed (`|sin(pi * cellsCrossed)|`), no squash.
2. Jumps (`player-moved` with style `jump`: the world sets it, `packages/world/src/sim/index.ts:83`; `w`, `f`, `G`, `/` and the rest): an arc whose height grows with distance, `clamp(6 + 1.5 * cells, 6, 20)` px (keep the long-jump rule that vertical jumps over 6 rows flatten). Duration `clamp(160 + 15 * cells, 160, 360)` ms, ease-in-out (sine). Keep `blink` for `%` and `scroll` for `<C-d>`/`<C-u>` as they are, with the new jump timing.
3. Jump trail: while a jump plays, 3 ghost copies of the player sprite along the path (alpha 0.35, 0.25, 0.15), fading out with the jump; on landing, a small squash (scaleY 0.8 -> 1, scaleX 1.15 -> 1, 90 ms). This shows the cells the jump skipped.
4. Bumps: the player leans 4 px toward the blocked cell (direction from the cursor to `bump.at`) and back, 140 ms, ease-out; a 2-3 px dust puff at the edge of the blocked cell. Keep the existing bump tint for the stages that have it.
5. Input never waits and never snaps:
   - Unfinished keys (a count, `f`, `d`, `"a`, `:`) must not cut the current movement short.
   - A finished command that arrives while the player is still moving starts its animation from the player's drawn position, not from the previous destination. If the drawn position is more than 1 cell behind the cursor, move it to 1 cell behind first (so holding `j` never lags more than a cell).
   - Stage changes, restores (`:e!`, death, Continue) and reduced motion still snap at once (`finish()`), and the camera snaps with them.
6. Camera: it follows the eased ground position of the move (no bob, no arc), as now; on a jump it leads slightly toward the landing cell (at most a quarter of the jump distance, eased). No camera shake anywhere.
7. Idle: each actor gets its own phase (a stable offset from its id) on a 600 ms idle cycle, so the map does not twitch in unison; water and other animated tiles keep one shared slower cycle (900 ms).
8. Edits (rows, deletion, transfer, stamp, archive fold, unwriting): raise the shared budget from 90 to 180 ms. Keep their choreography.
9. Reduced motion: no trail, no squash, no bob, no lean; moves snap as today.

## Limits
- No animation longer than 420 ms, except the existing cutscene and death effects.
- No new dependency. No change to `FieldFrame`, the world events or anything in `packages/**`.
- 60 fps on the Labyrinth and the Last Page at 1280x800 (check the trail does not allocate sprites per frame: pool them).
