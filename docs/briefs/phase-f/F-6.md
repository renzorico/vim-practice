2. Three seals say "the Notary" too: `9j .`, `j .`, `j .`: `.` repeats the whole `ci"` from anywhere on the line (a `ct"` would need `0f"l` first every time).
3. `gg 2j 3f" ci"the Readers<Esc>`: the second party.
4. Clause 1's brackets nest: `(by order (of the Notary))`. `2j 0f( ci(unless they want to<Esc>`: standing on the outer `(`, `ci(` takes everything inside it; `ct)` would stop at the inner `)`.
5. Clause 2: `j 0f[ ci[until the Scribe returns<Esc>`.

7. Witness: `f< citTilde<Esc>`. The trap: the whole contract is inside `<contract>`, so `cit` from outside `<name>` rewrites everything (`u` undoes it).
8. Notes: `} j cipGuardian of the caves: the Minotaur.<Esc>`: `cip` rewrites the paragraph and keeps its blank row. This names the Minotaur (World 6, MASTERPLAN 3.3).
