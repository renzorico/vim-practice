## The idea (proven in the sim)
The vault under the archive. The Notary sits at his desk on the top row, out of reach, and signs: every 2 turns he puts a clause `"s" ` after himself (behaviour `put`, every 2). At seven clauses the vault is his for good: the attempt is lost (`hit` 3, as the Counterfeiter's fuse). Below, three locks on three vault rows, each next to the stair (column 0):
- a lock `[kkk]`: `w` lands on the keys, `da[` takes the lock whole;
- a seal `{kkk}`: `da{`;
- a tag `<w>wwww</w>`: `dit` empties it and keeps the tag (the vault wants the tag, not the wax).
`0 2j w` goes from one lock to the next. The goal is one multi-row text trigger on the vault anchor at (2,0), covering all three lock rows.
Measured: par 11, `j w da[ 0 2j w da{ 0 2j w dit` (5 clauses when it opens). With the Archive commands (no `[`, `{` or tag objects) the route is 15 (`%` + `d%` for the lock and the seal, `f> l dt<` for the tag): the fuse fires at turn 14 first. Fighting him is not the lesson: his row cannot be reached (`k` from the stair bumps, `gg`/`2G` bump).
