## The idea (proven in the engine)
After the counting house, the harbourmaster's ledger: the clerk's copies are still written in it. Pure text. World 4's grammar fixes it, and the new pieces are `c` (change: delete, then type) and the line tools `P` and `yy`.
1. Keeper: `j 2w`, then `ct,Ream<Esc>`: change up to the comma.
2. The Lighthouse keeper's order (the quest chain: the letter from World 1) was written at the bottom; it belongs on top. `G dd gg P`: `P` puts the line above you, which `p` cannot do on the first line.
3. Crates: rope and oil are in the wrong order. `5j ddp` swaps them.
4. "copied" becomes "real" three times. `$ b cwreal<Esc>`, then `k b .` and `2j b .`: `.` repeats the whole change (with `dw` + `i`, `.` would repeat only the typing). The three words sit in the same column on purpose.
5. Ships: `} 2j 2w Cthe ferry, today<Esc>`; `yyp` copies the line, `2w ct,the rope boat<Esc>`.
6. Signed: `j 0 2w CTilde, for Ream<Esc>`.
Route (104 keys, checked against the target in the engine): `j2wct,Ream<Esc>GddggP5jddp$bcwreal<Esc>kb.2jb.}2j2wCthe ferry, today<Esc>yyp2wct,the rope boat<Esc>j02wCTilde, for Ream<Esc>`.
