## The idea (proven in the sim)
A hollow tree, 37 rows tall. Shut keeps the bottom gate, Ope the top one. They were one keeper before the Flicker; now each believes he is alone, and the gate needs both.
- `%`: the gate marks are real brackets in the buffer. Standing on the outer `)` at (36,3), `%` lands on its partner `(` at (0,3), and back. The inner `)` at (36,2) pairs with the `(` at (15,2), a sealed room with a Scribe note: `%` finds its own partner, not the nearest bracket.
- `<C-d>`/`<C-u>`: the hollow (column 5) has a landing every 6 rows and open air between. `Stage.windowHeight` is 12, so `<C-d>` moves exactly 6: landing to landing. `6j` bumps (it walks through air); `3<C-d>` bumps.
- The errand: Ope will not believe in his brother while the trunk lamps are dark. The lower lamp (row 24) must be lit first, then the upper (row 12), "the flame climbs". From the top: `<C-d>` x4, `<C-u>` x2, `<C-d>` x4.
- Two Blots patrol the landings on rows 6 and 30 (three cells wide; the middle cell is the hollow column). Landing on one costs a heart.
Learner route: `h <CR> % <CR> 2l <C-d>x4 <C-u>x2 <C-d>x4 2h <CR> % <CR>` (15 turns).
Known and accepted (Vim freedom): `1G`, `25G`, `65%` and counts on `<C-d>` also get around. Do not block them.

## 4. Sprites and tiles (drawn in code, same style)

## 5. On screen
- `%` is a blink, not an arc: the player fades out on the mark (30 ms) and in on the partner (30 ms), with a brass glint on both; the camera cuts to the partner within the step budget.
- `<C-d>`/`<C-u>`: the player drops/climbs the hollow fast, and the camera scrolls exactly half a screen (the player keeps the same screen row where the map allows).
- A failed `%` (not on a bracket and none ahead on the row) is an engine error: bump, no turn.
