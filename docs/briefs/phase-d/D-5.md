## The idea (proven in the engine)
Past the Twin Gates, the ferry. Its manifest is wrong, and the ferry won't sail on a wrong manifest. Pure text, like the Ledger: World 3's motions get you to the right place, World 2's edits fix it.
1. Title: "Wordwoood" has an o too many. `fo` lands on the first o, `;` on the next, `x`.
2. Passengers: the twins are coming. `0` puts you on the `(`, `%` jumps to its `)`, `i, Shut, Ope<Esc>`.
3. Cargo: `}` to the blank line, `3j`, `f2` then `r4`: four coils of rope.
4. Ink is banned: `j` then `dd`.
5. Notes: `G`, `f:` `l` `D`, `a today.<Esc>`. Then `k`, `t.` stops before the full stop, `a, on three<Esc>`.
Route `fo;xj0%i, Shut, Ope<Esc>}3jf2r4jddGf:lDa today.<Esc>kt.a, on three<Esc>`: 59 keys, checked against the target in the engine. Any route that reaches the target is a clear (Vim freedom).
