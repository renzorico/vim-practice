## Goal
After the Mason's Ledger (2-4), the player enters the Wordwood. Branches hang in open air; walking cannot cross a gap, but `f`, `t`, `;` and `,` only test the cell they land on:
1. Tier A (row 1): `fH` lands on the rope ladder at the far end, across both gaps. `f*` then `;` hops knot to knot.
2. Tier B (row 3), right to left: `,` repeats the last find backwards (after `fH`, it lands on the far ladder).
3. Tier C (row 5): thorns past the gap. `t@` stops one cell short of them, on the only branch cell over there.
4. Tier D (row 7): `fD` to the lift platform; Enter at the lift clears the stage.
Proven in the sim (motions only): par 7, `fH 2j , 2j t@ 2j fD`, 3 hearts; without `f t ; ,` the best route is 13 turns.
