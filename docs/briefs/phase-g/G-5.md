## The idea (proven in the sim)
The labyrinth. "Found by search, never by walking" (MASTERPLAN 3.3). The Minotaur is never seen: his hoofprints fill the top row (behaviour `put` "M" every 2 turns after his lair; the row is rock past him, and prints are not walkable). At six prints he has found you: the attempt is lost (`hit` 3, as the Counterfeiter and the Notary).
Three rune levers `x`, `y`, `z` sit in sealed chambers (a walking reachability check proves no `hjkl` path reaches them, or the stair from them). Each rune has a false copy in another chamber. The real one is always behind you, so `?` (backward) finds it first and `/` finds the false one first.
- `ma` on the stair (5,3). `?x<CR>`: the real x (4,8). `?y<CR>`: the real y (2,9). `?z<CR>`: wraps to the real z (8,2). Each lever lights only in order (chained `enter` triggers, as the Mouth's crystals).
- `` `a ``: home to the stair; arriving there after the third lever opens the way out (an `enter` trigger on the stair, `onlyIf` the third lever).
- Without a mark the player is stuck in a sealed chamber; the Minotaur's prints then end the attempt, and the retry starts clean. That is the fuse's job here: it turns "stuck" into a restart.
Measured: par 5, `ma ?x<CR> ?y<CR> ?z<CR> `a` (2 prints). With `/` and `n` instead of `?` the route is 8 (4 prints). Without search (World 5 tools) the chambers cannot be reached at all. Fuse at turn 12.
