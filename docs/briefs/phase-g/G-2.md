## The idea (proven in the sim)
The mouth of the Echo Caves, under the Library. Dark: the player sees only a few cells around them. Three crystals sit in sealed pockets of rock (a walking reachability check proves no `hjkl` path reaches them, or the Echo beside the top one). They must be lit from the deepest up, as the Twin Gates' lamps were lit in order.
- `?x<CR>` searches backwards for a crystal: from the mouth it wraps round to the deepest one, (5,14). Landing there lights it.
- `n` repeats the search the same way: the middle crystal (3,13), then the top one (1,13).
- `l` onto the ledge, next to the Echo at (1,15); `<CR>`.
- `/x<CR>` finds the top crystal first: it lights nothing (out of order), and the player turns round with `N`/`?`.
Measured: par 4, `?x<CR> n n l <CR>`. The light triggers are `enter` triggers on the crystal cells, chained with `onlyIf` (deep, then middle, then top).
Known and accepted: crystals are the only word characters in the cave, so a counted `w` (or `b`) also lands on them in one turn each. In the dark the player cannot count words they cannot see; search is the only practical tool (as in 4-2, where only `p` could create text). Do not try to block counted word motions.
