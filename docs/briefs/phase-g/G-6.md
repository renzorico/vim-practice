## The idea (proven in the engine)
The Scribe's study, at last, and she is not in it. Her index of the things she left for the player is a table, and every entry says "lost". Block visual is the lesson: edit a column on every row at once.
1. `2j f|`, then `<C-v>7j9l d`: the shelf column goes from all eight rows in one command.
2. `$b <C-v>7j$ cfound<Esc>`: with `$` the block reaches every line's end; `c` types "found" on all eight.
3. `0 <C-v>7j I* <Esc>`: a star at the start of every row.
4. `} j V2j d`: three drafts of the Last Page go; the final one stays.
Route: 37 keys, checked against the target in the engine (the `solution` in the file). Without visual mode (column by column with `.`: `2jf|d10lj.j.j.j.j.j.j.7k$bcwfound<Esc>jb.jb.jb.jb.jb.jb.jb.7k0I* <Esc>j.j.j.j.j.j.j.}j3dd`) it is 81. Any route that reaches the target is a clear.
Note: a block of `9l` spans ten columns (inclusive), so the per-row equivalent is `d10l`.
