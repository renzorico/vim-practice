## The idea (proven in the sim)
The echo gallery: a row of sealed chambers in the rock, each holding carved words. The Scribe's words echo from chamber to chamber, and each chamber names the next word to follow. Decoys hold longer words that contain the trail word (`inkwell`, `maps`, `keys`).
- You start on `ink`. `*` searches for the whole word under the cursor: it skips `inkwell` and lands on the next `ink`, in the chamber that also holds `map`.
- `w` steps onto the `=` before `map`; `*` there takes the next keyword on the line (Vim), so it searches `map`: it skips `maps` and lands in the chamber with `key`. `w`, `*` again: the last chamber.
- `e` to the end of `key`, next to the Echo at (7,5); `<CR>`.
- `/ink<CR>` (World 6's first tool) stops on `inkwell` first, and every step needs an `n` more.
Measured: par 6, `* w * w * e <CR>`. With the Mouth commands (`/ ? n N`, no `*`/`#`) the route is 9 or more (`/ink<CR> n w /map<CR> n ...`). Darkness as in the Mouth (reuse it; the gallery is dark too).
