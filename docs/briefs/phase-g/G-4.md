## The idea (proven in the sim)
A fork in a long tunnel. The Echo waits at the fork and needs two things from sealed pockets in the rock: a crystal and lamp oil. Search gets you into a pocket (`/x<CR>`, World 6's first tool). Nothing gets you out: the tunnel is plain floor, so there is nothing unique to search for on the way back, `gg`/`G` land in rock, and no `hjkl` path leaves a pocket (a walking reachability check proves both pockets are sealed).
- `ma` marks the fork before leaving. `/x<CR>` into the crystal pocket (landing on the crystal collects it). `` `a `` jumps back to the fork from anywhere.
- `/o<CR>` into the oil pocket, `` `a `` back. `<CR>` to the Echo beside the fork.
- Without a mark, the player is stuck in a pocket: `:e!` starts over (the HUD shows it). The intro says to mark the fork first; the death hint is not needed (nothing kills here).
Measured: par 5, `ma /x<CR> `a /o<CR> `a <CR>`. With the gallery commands (no marks) there is no practical route back (a search pattern that only matches the tunnel exists in principle, `?#.` style; accepted as Vim freedom, do not block it).
